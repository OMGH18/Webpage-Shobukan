What you play in Shobukan

Two group modes for game nights, two solo modes to play every day.

Undercover

Everyone gets a secret word from a manga, except the impostor, whose word is close but different. Take turns giving a clue, then vote to unmask the impostor. Watch out for Mister White, who doesn't even know their word.

3 to 8 players · same phone or online

Otaku Quiz

One manga question, four answers, only one is right. Play solo to rank up, with friends on the same phone, or challenge other players online.

Solo or 2 to 12 players

Daily

Anime Grid

A 9-cell grid where each cell crosses a manga with a criterion. Find a character for every cell: the rarer your answer, the higher your score. Global leaderboard, new grid every day.

Solo · daily leaderboard

Adventure

Expedition

A solo adventure in chapters where your manga knowledge is your only weapon: answer right to defeat enemies and push as far as you can.

Solo · chapter progression

Creator

FAQ

Is Shobukan free?

Yes. Every mode and all 18 manga universes are free to play, with ads. The optional Premium subscription (€10 per month or €90 per year, reference prices in France) removes ads and unlocks unlimited online hosting.

How many players can play?

From 1 to 12 depending on the mode: Otaku Quiz is played solo or with up to 12 players, Undercover with 3 to 8 players. Anime Grid and Expedition are solo.

Can you play on a single phone?

Yes. In local mode the phone is passed around. Online, each player uses their own phone and joins with a 6-character code.

Which manga are in the game?

18 universes: Naruto, One Piece, Dragon Ball, My Hero Academia, Bleach, Jujutsu Kaisen, Demon Slayer, Attack on Titan, Hunter x Hunter, Death Note, JoJo, Fullmetal Alchemist, Chainsaw Man, One Punch Man, Spy x Family, Tokyo Ghoul, Black Clover and Kingdom, plus an all-universes mix.

Which phones is it available on?

iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play), in English and French.
