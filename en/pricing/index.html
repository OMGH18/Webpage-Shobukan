Pricing

How much does Shobukan cost?

Shobukan is free: every mode and all 18 manga universes can be played without paying, with ads. The optional Premium subscription costs €10 per month or €90 per year in France. It removes ads and makes online hosting unlimited.

Creator credits

A creator credit costs €1.99 and lets you create one custom pack (your own questions or words). Once created, anyone can play and share your pack for free. Premium includes 1 credit.

One Premium covers the whole group

When a Premium player hosts an online game, everyone at the table plays without ads, even if they are not subscribed.
